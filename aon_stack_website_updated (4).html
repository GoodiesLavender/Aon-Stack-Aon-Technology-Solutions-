FAQ

Common questions answered clearly

How does payment work?
You are redirected to a secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe handles all payment processing — no card details are collected on this website. Apple Pay may appear automatically for eligible customers on supported Apple devices and browsers. Stripe accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
What's the Starter Setup launch price?
Starter Setup is regularly priced at $499 one-time. The first 5 customers receive a $100 discount, bringing the launch price to $399. Once the 5 launch spots are filled, pricing returns to $499.
When does the 7–10 day timeline start?
The 7–10 business day timeline begins after two things are complete: (1) payment is confirmed, and (2) your onboarding form is fully submitted with all required business information, content, and access details. Delays may occur if required information is not provided on time, approvals are delayed, or additional requests are made outside the package scope.
What corrections can I request in the preview?
The Starter Setup includes one round of basic corrections related to setup accuracy — for example: fixing a phone number, updating business hours, correcting a service description, adding a missing FAQ, or fixing a typo. Additional requests such as new website pages, advanced design changes, new branding, SEO campaigns, or other work outside the package scope may be quoted separately.
How does Growth Care cancellation work?
Growth Care is a month-to-month subscription. You may cancel anytime before your next billing cycle through your customer account dashboard. After cancellation, your service remains active until the end of your current paid billing period. No future charges will be made after cancellation. All work already delivered (content updates, social posts, chatbot updates) remains yours.
Can I add Growth Care after Starter Setup?
Yes. If you purchase Starter Setup, you qualify for Growth Care at the discounted add-on price of $199/month — saving $100 every month compared to the $299/month standalone rate. You can add it at any time after your Starter Setup is complete.
What is not included in these packages?
Starter Setup does not include ongoing marketing, SEO campaigns, content creation, ad management, daily support, branding redesigns, or advanced custom development. Growth Care does not include full social media management, paid ads management, video editing, daily posting, unlimited support, advanced SEO campaigns, or large website redesigns. Additional work outside the package scope may be quoted separately before any work begins.
What happens if I don't provide my business information on time?
The 7–10 business day delivery timeline depends on receiving complete business information, content, photos, access credentials, and approvals. If required information is not provided promptly, the timeline may be extended. The setup does not begin until the onboarding form is fully submitted.
